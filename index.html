I’m Prince, a 14-year-old builder.

I’m deeply interested in mathematics and physics. I like understanding things from first principles, not just knowing that something works, but trying to understand why it works.

Right now, I’m working on autonomous drones.

I’m interested in the complete problem: flight, propulsion, control systems, electronics, sensors, navigation, software, and the physics that connects all of them. My goal is not simply to make a drone fly. I want to understand how an autonomous machine can perceive its environment, make decisions, and move through the real world reliably.

A lot of my learning happens through building. I experiment with electronics, robotics, programming, flight systems, and whatever else helps me turn an idea into something real.

I enjoy the point where physics becomes engineering, where an equation becomes a design, a circuit becomes a system, and an idea becomes a working machine.

I’m still at the beginning of this journey. There is a lot I don’t know yet, and that is exactly what makes it interesting.

I want to understand deeply, build relentlessly, and see how far an idea can go when it is actually made real.
